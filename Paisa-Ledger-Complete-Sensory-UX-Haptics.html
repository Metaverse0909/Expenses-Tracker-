Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

LEDVIX is designed as an offline-first personal finance application. Information you enter, such as transactions, income, expenses, budgets, IOUs, tasks, settings and receipt images, is intended to remain on your device in the offline version.

Local storage

Transaction metadata and application settings use local device storage. Receipt images are handled separately through local browser/device storage.

No sale of financial information

LEDVIX does not sell your personal financial information.

Data you enter

  • Income and expense records
  • Amounts, dates, notes, categories and payment methods
  • Lent and borrowed IOUs
  • Tasks and reminders
  • Monthly income and working hours
  • Budgets, subscriptions and goals
  • Receipt images
  • Profile, security and app preferences

Future changes

If a future version adds cloud sync, analytics, advertising, external AI services or other remote processing, this policy and the applicable Play Store data declarations will be updated before those features are distributed.

Google Play Data Safety Summary

The final Play Store Data Safety declaration must match the exact production build and any third-party SDK behavior.

For the current offline-first design, financial records are intended for local device storage rather than routine transmission to an LEDVIX server.

Exported backups are user-created files. Treat them as sensitive because they may contain financial records and receipt information.

Declaration status: To be finalized against the release APK/AAB before Play Store submission.

Financial Feature Declaration

LEDVIX provides personal finance tracking and calculation tools. Features include income and expense logging, budgets, recurring/subscription detection, Financial Health Score, Time-to-Money calculations, spending trends and IOU tracking.

LEDVIX does not present these calculations as professional financial, investment, tax, accounting or legal advice.

Users remain responsible for financial decisions and for checking records before relying on them.

Permissions & Device Access

Permissions are requested for optional features rather than ordinary transaction logging.

Camera

Used when you choose to scan/import an IOU QR code.

Clipboard

Used when you choose the Bank SMS paste/parser action. The parser processes text you provide to extract transaction details.

Files

Used when you choose import/export or backup actions supported by the app.

Biometrics / passcode

Used only when the related security feature is enabled and supported by the device/application build.

QR IOU Sharing

When you generate an IOU QR code, the payload may contain the IOU type, amount, person name, date, note and status.

The QR is intended for sharing by your action. Do not share an IOU QR code with people you do not trust.

LEDVIX does not independently verify whether an IOU is legally enforceable.

Backup, Export & Data Deletion

Backup

Exported files may contain sensitive financial information and receipt data. Store them securely.

Deletion

Use the app's available delete, reset or wipe controls to remove locally stored records. Clearing app/device storage also removes locally stored application data.

Important

Deleting local data is intended to be permanent for the deleted records. Create a backup first if you need to preserve your information.

AI Coach & Savage Mode

The Coach uses predefined, rule-based messages to provide motivational spending feedback. Savage Mode uses humorous Hinglish roast-style messages; Gentle Mode uses softer reminders.

Coach messages are informational and motivational. They are not professional financial advice.

Terms of Use & Disclaimer

Use LEDVIX for personal organization and financial awareness. You are responsible for the accuracy of information you enter, imported, or share.

Financial Health Score, Time-to-Money, recurring expense estimates and spending comparisons are calculations based on available app data. Results are estimates and should not be treated as guaranteed financial outcomes.

LEDVIX does not verify bank messages, receipts, IOUs or imported records. Review imported data before relying on it.

Children's Privacy

LEDVIX is designed as a general personal finance management application and is not specifically directed toward children.
